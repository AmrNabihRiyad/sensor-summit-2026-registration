ABOUT THE SUMMIT

Connecting Sensing Technologies with Digital Innovation

Sensors Summit 2026 is an initiative organized by the IEEE Sensors Council Egypt Chapter to encourage knowledge exchange, academic engagement, and networking around emerging sensing technologies.

This edition explores Digital Twin in Biosensing Applications, highlighting the intersection of sensor technologies, digital representations, data-driven analysis, and biosensing research.

The summit provides an opportunity for participants to engage with a specialized technical topic, connect with members of the academic and engineering communities, and explore potential directions for research and innovation.

SUMMIT THEME

Digital Twin in Biosensing Applications

A digital twin is a digital representation of a physical system that can be informed by data from that system. In biosensing, this concept opens opportunities to explore how sensor measurements, digital models, and computational analysis can work together.

Potential areas of exploration include:

  • Digital representations of biosensing systems.
  • Sensor data integration and analysis.
  • Monitoring and modelling of biological processes.
  • Research opportunities in data-driven biosensing.
  • Emerging applications and challenges in digital twin technologies.

The summit theme offers a focused opportunity to explore these concepts and their relevance to the future of biosensing.
