The Summit

Ideas that sense what comes next.

IEEE Sensors Summit 2026 explores the evolving relationship between sensing technologies and digital twins in biosensors. The summit creates space for research, practical innovation, and collaboration across the sensing community.

Join conversations around the technologies and ideas connecting real-world signals with new ways to understand and respond to them.

Participation rules

Current registration information from the event application. Organizers should review this section against the final approved event rules before launch.

Application review

Applications are reviewed by the organizing team. Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance; the final decision is sent by email.

Attendance

Select one or both event days when applying. Day 1 is the Summit Talks & Keynotes; Day 2 is the Hands-on Workshop.

Day 2 eligibility

The workshop is intended for senior undergraduate students, Master's students, PhD students, and early-career PhD holders (PhD awarded in 2024 or later). Eligibility is reviewed by organizers.

Fees and transport

The existing application lists a 200 EGP registration fee and an additional 100 EGP for requested transportation.

Payment review

Refund information
